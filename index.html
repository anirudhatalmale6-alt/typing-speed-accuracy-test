How these numbers are worked out
  • Gross WPM = (characters typed ÷ 5) ÷ minutes elapsed. The standard "one word = 5 characters" rule.
  • Net WPM = Gross WPM − (errors left uncorrected ÷ minutes elapsed). Never shown below zero.
  • Keystroke accuracy = correct keys ÷ all keys pressed. A mistake counts even if you fix it afterwards.
  • Final text accuracy = characters that match the passage ÷ characters typed, judged on what is on screen at the end.
  • The clock starts on your first keystroke and is read from the browser's monotonic clock, so it does not drift if the tab stutters.
